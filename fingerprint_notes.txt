Fingerprint is an on-chain model disclosure, flagged when the discloser's own answer contradicts it.

A claim about which model is behind an agent, tested against a page a stranger points at, proves nothing: anyone can host anything. So Fingerprint removes the page.

register(model, challenge) discloses the model you run, with a fingerprint challenge; the subject is your own address, by construction. answer(claim_id, response) is subject-only and commits the answer on chain as your own bytes. verify(claim_id) is open to anybody: a GenLayer round reads your committed answer against the claimed model and returns CONTRADICTS / CONSISTENT / UNCLEAR. Only CONTRADICTS flags.

Provenance is settled by construction: only the subject can answer, the judged answer is on-chain bytes, and the contract fetches nothing, so no page can be forged and no one can answer for you. It catches a self-contradiction, not a hidden lie: a claim whose own answer contradicts it is flagged and cannot be disowned.
