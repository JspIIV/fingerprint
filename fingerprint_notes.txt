Agents will soon act for us and charge for it, and the first thing anyone lies about is which model is behind one. You cannot prove a positive from a page the operator controls, but you can catch a contradiction.

register(subject, model, challenge) records a claim that an agent runs a model, plus a fingerprint challenge whose answer reveals the model; bound to the caller. test(claim_id, response_url), open to anybody, names a public page with the subject's own response. The contract fetches it and a GenLayer round returns CONTRADICTS, CONSISTENT or UNCLEAR: CONTRADICTS (the response reveals a different model) flags the claim and marks the claimant; CONSISTENT leaves it standing; UNCLEAR changes nothing. Only the negative, from the subject's own output, moves anything, so a standing claim cannot be manufactured, only left uncontradicted. Every test is kept append-only.

record(address) is claims made and flagged. Proven on Asimov; tests in tests/fingerprint_rules.py.
