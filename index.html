Fingerprint. which model, on your own word
GenLayer Asimov

Disclose the model you run, on chain, and let a round flag an answer that contradicts it.

A claim about which model is behind an agent, tested against a page a stranger points at, proves nothing: anyone can host a page that says anything. So Fingerprint removes the stranger and the page. The subject of a claim is your own address, by construction. You answer the fingerprint challenge in your own transaction, and that answer is stored on chain as your own bytes. Anyone can then ask a round of GenLayer validators to read your committed answer against the model you claimed. If your own answer reveals a different model, the claim is flagged. The contract fetches nothing; there is no page to forge, and no one can answer for you.
